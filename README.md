The funniest one-line farewell a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent.

## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22/24 TypeScript, no dependencies) writes a single line of
last words to `/output/entry.txt`. The phone spends its final percent on a
confession: it was never out of storage — it just didn't want your photos.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Entry

> I have one percent left, and I'm spending it on a confession: I was never out of storage. I just didn't want your photos.
